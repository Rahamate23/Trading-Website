PAPERLINE PRINTING PAPER WEBSITE

FILES
- index.html  : Home page
- papers.html : Full paper catalogue page
- style.css   : Website design and responsive/mobile styling
- script.js   : Product catalogue, search, category filters and WhatsApp enquiries

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP folder.
2. Open the folder in Visual Studio Code.
3. Double-click index.html, or right-click it in VS Code and choose Open with Live Server (if installed).
4. Click Our papers / Explore our collection to open the separate catalogue page.

IMPORTANT CUSTOMISATION
1. Open script.js.
2. Find: const WHATSAPP_NUMBER = '9779800000000';
3. Replace the sample number with your own WhatsApp number, including country code but without +, spaces or dashes.
4. Change PAPERLINE in index.html and papers.html to your business name.
5. Edit the products array in script.js to update paper names, GSM, finish and descriptions. Prices are not displayed because they should be confirmed by your business.

NOTE
This is a front-end starter website. It does not yet include a database, admin panel, online payments, stock management or automatic order processing. WhatsApp enquiry buttons work after you add your real number. Product examples/specifications are placeholders; confirm your actual inventory and supplier specifications before publishing.
